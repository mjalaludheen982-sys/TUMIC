WELCOME TO OUR COLLEGE

Thrippanachi Usthad
Memorial Islamic College

“The institute carries forward the old Ponnani tradition of scholar training.”

Established in 2013, the college is a residential Islamic educational institution at Kodimaram, South Thrippanachi, Kerala, affiliated with Jamia Nooriyya Arabiyya.

OUR MISSION

Mission

To develop an Islamic educational framework in order to prepare religious scientists (‘Ulama) who acquire, practice and propagate Islam, being alert of their ultimate responsibilities towards Almighty Allah and towards themselves, family, institution, society, surroundings and private and public resources and to make them capable of competing the shifting trends of modern education.

✦
OUR VISION

Vision

TUMIC aims to be the bastion of expertise in Islamic education that they can restore the dynamic role and educational superiority of past Muslim society in all intellectual programs and that seeks to rekindle the spirit of scholarship in Islamic sciences in the era of ever-changing society due to the innovative growth of modern sciences.
